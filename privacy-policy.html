Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Deka Works Studio collects, uses, and protects information when you visit dekaworks.com, contact us, or sign up to test our apps.

1. Scope

This policy covers the dekaworks.com website only. Each of our apps (Kinties, The Bride Society, FloArte, and Bildva) has its own privacy policy, available inside the app and on its app store or website listing.

2. Information You Give Us

We collect information you choose to send us, including:

  • Your name, email address, and message when you use our contact form or email us
  • Your name, email address, device type, and reason for applying when you sign up to be an app tester

3. Information Collected Automatically

We use Plausible Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages are viewed and which links are clicked. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect information that identifies you personally.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Reply to your messages and questions
  • Invite you to test early releases of our apps and ask for your feedback
  • Understand how our website is used so we can improve it
  • Keep our website and forms secure and free of spam
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website, such as our website host, our analytics provider, and the service that delivers contact form messages to our inbox, and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Data Retention

We keep messages and tester signups only as long as we need them to respond to you, run our testing programs, or meet legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email info@dekaworks.com.

9. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website links to app stores and other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date below shows when it last changed.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Deka Works Studio at info@dekaworks.com.

Effective date: October 31, 2026
