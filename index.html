Our Apps

Each app starts with a real problem we have lived through, then gets shaped by the people who use it.

Kinties

Android · Closed testing

A private space for families, away from corporate social media. Share births, graduations, and family news, and plan reunions together in one place.

  • Invite-only family groups, so only family can see and share
  • Reunion tools: RSVPs, budget, polls, and activities
  • Shared photo vault organized by year, plus a family video room

The Bride Society

Android · In development

A wedding planning app with Glimmer, a built-in AI helper that guides couples from the first idea to the big day.

  • Plan colors, flowers, attire, and decor around your budget
  • Find local shops near your venue
  • Wedding-day mode for helpers and vendors

FloArte

Android · Closed testing

A visual thinking tool that maps projects, systems, and processes as clear flowcharts, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

  • Built for planning apps, products, and team processes
  • Keeps every step and decision in one view
  • Designed for engineering, product, and onboarding teams

Bildva

Web · Live

A guided creation platform where complete beginners build a real app while learning the fundamentals, without feeling lost, overwhelmed, or not technical enough.

  • Teaches by doing through real app creation, not lectures
  • AI mentor adapts explanations to pace, confidence, and curiosity
  • Users finish with a working app they can confidently show off

About

Deka Works Studio is an independent app studio. We build our apps one step at a time, launch them on Android first, and keep improving them based on what our users tell us.

FAQ

What does Deka Works Studio make?

We design, build, and run our own apps: Kinties for families, The Bride Society for couples planning a wedding, FloArte for mapping projects and processes, and Bildva for first-time app builders.

Are your apps on iPhone?

Our apps launch on Android first. iPhone versions are planned for later.

How do I get help with one of your apps?

Use the support email listed inside the app or on its Google Play page, or contact us at info@dekaworks.com and we will point you in the right direction.

How can I test your apps before launch?

Sign up on our App Testers page. We invite testers to early releases and ask for honest feedback.

Do you build apps for other businesses?

Not right now. We are focused on building and improving our own apps.
