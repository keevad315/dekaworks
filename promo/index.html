Deka Works Studio is an independent app studio. We design, build and run our own apps for real life: staying close to family, planning a wedding, finishing a big project and getting an app out into the world.

Where our apps come from

Every app starts with a real problem.

Kinties started because family news only reached whoever saw it on Facebook, and the rest of the family kept missing reunions and big moments.

FloArte started because first-time builders kept losing track of where they were and what came next, and gave up on good ideas.

Launchkins started because getting an app through testing is hard and lonely, and nobody should have to pay just to be taken seriously.
