The Deka Works story

It started with missing out.

Chapter one

The news was there. Just not for everyone.

Family reunion · posted on Facebook
New baby! · posted on Facebook
"Wait, when was this?" · seen two weeks later

Family news lived on social media. If you weren't scrolling, nobody remembered to tell you. Reunions, births, big moments: missed.

Chapter two

Every day, helping people keep up.

Our founder works in tech support, helping people every day, many of them seniors, with the technology that keeps them connected, like a simple video call with family.

"So many apps are built to grab attention. So few are built to actually help."

Chapter three

Apps for brains that work differently.

Some minds jump from idea to idea, work in bursts, and give up when they have to hunt for things. Most apps make that harder. Deka Works builds for those brains: one job per screen, nothing to search for.

Chapter four

Building apps was its own maze.

Tool after tool kept breaking the code. It was easy to lose track of where you were and what came next. That's the moment most first-time builders give up.

Instead of giving up, she mapped the way.

FloArte was born to answer two questions: where am I, and what's next?

Chapter five

A home for family, away from the noise.

Then came the app closest to her heart: Kinties, a private, calm place just for family. No strangers, no ads, no drama, so nobody misses a family moment again.

Come home from social media.

Chapter six

Nobody should launch alone.

Getting an app through testing is hard, lonely work, and nobody should have to pay just to be taken seriously. So came Launchkins: developers testing each other's apps, where nobody gets left behind.
